### Product Recap
- Goal: match members to relevant SDC/CivicHub opportunities (events, volunteer roles, jobs, petitions) instead of mass blasts to 900-person list.
- Hypothesis: more relevant opportunities drive more engagement, feeding a loop toward membership, donations, or volunteering.

### Email Conversion Data
- Ride for Refuge 2025 launch email: 29% open, 3% click, only 7 unique clicks to the event out of ~850 recipients.
- 2026 single-focus email lifted opens but clicks dropped; 12 unique Ride page clicks, one team signup, $1,000 in donations.
- Targeted resend to 72 prior participants still yielded only 7 unique clicks; unsubscribe rate stays low, so interest exists without conversion.
- Sohanya hypothesizes many members treat SDC as a trusted news/curation source rather than an action channel.

### Digital vs Personal Outreach
- December safe-tenting petition e-blast returned zero paper signatures; in-person pushing at events collected 900.
- Ride for Refuge stalled at $3-4K via email; personal 1:1 outreach pushed the total to nearly $15,000.
- Blue Sea (Ride licensor) confirms the same pattern nationally and advises tailored personal emails over slick campaigns.
- Personal outreach converts but does not scale; matching engine plus social sharing is the team's proposed leverage.

### Onboarding Survey Direction
- SDC lacks data on why members subscribe or how they want to engage; a survey is welcomed.
- Plan: build the onboarding/survey portion of the product first, run it with existing members, then hand-curate emails to test lift.
- Sohanya open to Blueprint's recommendations on survey length and questions.

### Next Steps
