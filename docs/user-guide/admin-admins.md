# Admins

For SDC admins. Any admin can invite other admins and remove them. Admins can see members' personal details, so give access only to staff who need it, using their own work email.

## Open the Admins page
1. Click **Admins** at the bottom of the left sidebar, above **Sign out**.

You see everyone who can sign in as an admin, with their name, email, the date they accepted the invite (**Added on**) and status. On a narrow screen the table scrolls sideways and the **Name** column stays in view:
- **Active**: they've opened their invite and can sign in.
- **Invited**: the invite is sent but not opened yet. Invite links expire after 7 days.
- **Not sent**: the invite email couldn't be sent.

People who haven't accepted yet (**Invited** or **Not sent**) are shown in grey and have a dash instead of a date.

Your own row shows **(You)** after your name.

To copy one person's email address, click it. A check mark shows that it was copied.

## Invite an admin
1. Click **Invite admin**.
2. Enter their **Name** and **Email**. Both are required.
3. Click **Send invite**.

Confetti shows that the invite is on its way, and the person is added as **Invited**. They get an email with a link to sign in. If the email is already an admin or already invited, the form says so.

## If an invite couldn't be sent
You see a message saying **We couldn't send the invite**, and we email you the details. The person appears as **Not sent**.
1. Click **Resend invite** on their row.
2. Check the **Email** carefully, and fix it if there's a typo.
3. Tick **I've checked that this email address is correct**.
4. Click **Resend invite**.

If it still can't be sent, the dialog stays open. Try a different address.

## Resend an invite
1. Find the person's row. Only people who are **Invited** or **Not sent** have the button.
2. Click **Resend invite** on their row.

You can resend an invite once a minute. Until then the option is greyed out and says **You can resend an invite once a minute.**

## Remove an admin
1. Click the remove button (the person with a minus sign, **Remove {name}**) on their row.
2. Type their full name in the box. The **Remove admin** button stays greyed out until it matches.
3. Click **Remove admin**.

They lose access on their next click. You can invite them again later. You can remove yourself too, and you lose access right away.

The last remaining admin can't be removed: **You can't remove the last admin. Invite another admin first.** Invite someone else first.

## Copy everyone's email address
1. Click **Copy all emails** at the top right.

All addresses, including people who haven't accepted yet, are copied separated by commas, ready to paste into an email. A message says how many were copied.
