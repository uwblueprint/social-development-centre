# Admins

For SDC admins. Every admin can do everything in the admin portal, including adding and removing other admins. Use **Admins** and **Paying members** at the top of the page to switch pages; **Sign out** is at the top right.

## Add an admin
1. Go to **Admins**.
2. Under **Add an admin**, enter their **Full name** and **Email address**.
3. Click **Send invite**.

They get an email with a sign-in link and show as **Invited** until they sign in, then **Active**. If they're already an admin, you'll see "This person is already an admin." If the email didn't send, you'll see "{name} was added, but the invite didn't send." Click **Resend link** on their row.

## Resend someone's sign-in link
Sign-in links work for 1 hour.

1. Go to **Admins**.
2. Click **Resend link** on their row. "Sent" shows beside it, or "Didn't send. Try again." if it failed.

People can also ask for a new link themselves on the admin sign-in page.

## Remove an admin
1. Go to **Admins**.
2. Click **Remove** on their row.
3. Click **Remove admin** to confirm, or **Cancel** to keep them.

They lose access to the admin portal right away, and "{name} is no longer an admin." shows above the list. This works for invited admins too. To give someone access again, add them as an admin.

## Leave the admin portal
1. Go to **Admins**.
2. Click **Leave** on your own row (it says "(you)").
3. Click **Leave** to confirm.

You're signed out and can't sign in to the admin portal again until another admin adds you back. If you're the only admin, there's no **Leave** button: add another admin first.
