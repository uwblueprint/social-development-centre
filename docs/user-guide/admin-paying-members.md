# Paying members

For SDC admins. Paying members can sign in to the members' area. They pay by e-transfer outside the app; add them here once their payment arrives.

## Add a paying member
1. Go to **Paying members**.
2. Under **Add a paying member**, enter their **Email address** and, if you know it, their **Full name**.
3. Click **Add paying member**.

They get an email with a sign-in link, and "{name} is now a paying member. We sent a sign-in link to {email}." shows under the form. They show as **Invited** until they sign in, then **Active**.

- If they're already on SDC's list as a general member, they become a paying member. Their existing name is kept.
- If they're already a paying member, nothing changes and you'll see "This person is already a paying member."
- If the email didn't send, they're still a paying member. Click **Resend link** on their row.

## Resend someone's sign-in link
Sign-in links work for 1 hour.

1. Go to **Paying members**.
2. Click **Resend link** on their row. "Sent" shows beside it, or "Didn't send. Try again." if it failed.

Members can also ask for a new link themselves on the sign-in page.

## Remove someone's paying access
1. Go to **Paying members**.
2. Click **Remove** on their row.
3. Click **Remove paying access** to confirm, or **Cancel** to keep them.

They stay on SDC's list as a general member but can't sign in to the members' area anymore, and "{name} is no longer a paying member." shows above the list. To give them access again, add them as a paying member.
