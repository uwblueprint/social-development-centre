# Fill in the intake form

For Ride for Refuge supporters. SDC offers you a complimentary membership in its new community, and
the form asks what you care about and how you'd like to be involved. It takes about two minutes.
There's no payment request.

You don't sign in, and there's no password. Anyone with the link can open it.

## Fill it in
1. Open the link SDC emailed you. You'll see a closed envelope.
2. Click the envelope to open your letter.
3. Read the letter and click **Begin survey**.
4. Enter your **First name**, **Last name** and **Email address**. All three are needed. Click **Next**.
5. Answer the five questions, one screen at a time. Click **Next** to carry on.
6. On the last question, click **Share my preferences**.

You'll see "Thanks for sharing!" once your answers are saved. That's the end — there's nothing else
to do, and you can close the page.

## Skip a question
Every question is optional. While you haven't chosen anything, the button reads **Skip for now**
instead of **Next** — click it to move on without answering. The button changes to **Next** as soon
as you choose something.

Your name and email are the exception: those three can't be skipped. Leave one out and the form
stays where it is and says what's missing, for example "Enter your first name." If your email is
missing an `@` or its ending, you'll see "Enter an email address like name@example.com."

## Change an answer before you finish
Click **Back** to return to the previous screen. What you chose is still there, and you can change
it before carrying on.

## If you'd rather not
On the letter, click **Not interested**. Nothing is saved, and you'll see "No problem". You can:
- click **Fill it in after all** to go straight to the questions, or
- close the page. The same link still works if you change your mind later.

Choosing **Not interested** doesn't tell SDC anything, and it doesn't stop SDC emailing you. To stop
the emails, reply to the email SDC sent you.

## If your answers don't save
You'll see "We couldn't save your answers. They're still here, so check your connection and choose
Share my preferences again." Nothing is lost: your answers stay on screen. Check your connection and
click **Share my preferences** again. Trying again won't create a second set of answers.

If it keeps failing, tell your SDC contact.

## After you've sent it
There's no way to change your answers once you've sent them, and no confirmation email. If something
is wrong or you'd like to add to it, contact SDC.
