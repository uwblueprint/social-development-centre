# Partners

For SDC admins. Partners are organizations that share opportunities with SDC. Each partner has one or more contacts who can sign in to manage the organization's opportunities.

## Find a partner or a person
1. Go to **Partners**.
2. Choose a tab: **Organizations** (current partners), **People** (everyone at current partners) or **Removed** (past partners).
3. Type in the search box. It matches organization names, contact names and emails.
4. Click a row to open its details on the right.

A **Pending** label means nobody at that organization has accepted their invitation yet (or, on the People tab, that this person hasn't).

## Invite a person from a new or existing partner
1. Go to **Partners** and click **Invite partner** (top right).
2. Enter the person's **Name** and **Email**.
3. Under **Organization**, start typing:
   - If the organization appears, choose it.
   - If it doesn't, choose **Create "<name you typed>"** to add it.
4. Click **Send invitation**.

The person gets an email with a link that works for 7 days. They appear as **Pending** until they accept.

**If the invitation can't be sent,** you'll see why, and the person is still listed with a **Retry** option. **If the email is already a current partner contact,** you'll be asked to use a different one.

## Add another person to an existing partner
1. Open the partner (click its row).
2. Click **Add person**. The organization is already filled in.
3. Enter their **Name** and **Email** and click **Send invitation**.

## Resend or cancel an invitation
1. Open the partner and find the pending person under **Contacts**.
2. Open the person's menu (**⋯**):
   - **Resend invitation** sends a new link. The old link stops working.
   - **Cancel invitation** deletes the invitation and the person's entry. If nobody else was ever active at that organization, the organization is deleted too.

## Edit a partner's details
1. Open the partner.
2. To rename the organization, change the name at the top of the panel and click **Save**.
3. To change a person's name or email, open their menu (**⋯**) and choose **Edit**.

Changing someone's email sends a new invitation to the new address. If they were already active, they keep their access; once they accept, they sign in with the new email.

## See a partner's opportunities
1. Open the partner.
2. Click **View opportunities**. The Opportunities list opens filtered to that partner.

## Remove a person who left a partner organization
1. Open the partner and find the person under **Contacts**.
2. Open their menu (**⋯**) and choose **Remove from organization**, then confirm.

They lose access to the partner portal right away. Their email can later be invited under a different organization.

You can't remove an organization's only contact; remove the organization instead.

## Move a person to a different partner
1. Remove them from their current organization (see above).
2. Invite their email under the new organization (see **Invite a person**).

## Remove a partner (the partnership ended or the organization dissolved)
1. Open the partner and click **Remove access**.
2. Read the confirmation and click to confirm.

What happens:
- **Right away:** everyone at that organization loses access to the partner portal, and its opportunities stop being recommended or included in new automated emails.
- **Over the next month:** each of its opportunities expires at its own end date or one month after removal, whichever comes first, and then disappears from the platform.
- **Can't be undone by SDC:** emails already sent can't be recalled, and links to the partner's own registration pages may keep working.

The partner moves to the **Removed** tab, where you can still see its details and history.

## Bring back a removed partner
1. Go to **Partners** > **Removed** and open the partner.
2. Update the organization name or contacts if anything changed.
3. Click **Reinvite** and confirm.

Every saved contact gets a new invitation, and the partner returns to **Organizations** as **Pending**. Opportunities that expired aren't republished automatically; each one needs to be reviewed first.
