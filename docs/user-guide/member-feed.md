# Member feed (paying members)

Where paying members find something to do. It lives at `/member` and has three places: **For you**, **Explore** and **Your opportunities**. On a phone they're the tabs along the bottom; on a tablet or computer they're along the top.

## Find something that suits you
1. Open **For you**. The large card at the top is your best match this week.
2. Click its button (for example **Register**, **Sign up to volunteer** or **Sign the petition**). The partner's own page opens in a new tab, where you finish signing up.
3. Come back to keep looking. **Closing soon** shows dates coming up this week, and there's one row for each topic you care about. Swipe or use the arrows to see more, or click **See all** to see every opportunity on that topic.

After you click, the button reads **Opened** with a check and the opportunity is added to **Your opportunities**. You can click it again to reopen the partner's page.

## Change what For you shows
1. Open **For you** and click **Edit interests** (or open the account menu, the circle with your initial, and choose **Edit interests**).
2. Choose as many topics as you like.
3. Click **Save interests**. **For you** re-sorts right away.

If you unselect every topic, **For you** asks you to choose interests again.

## Browse everything and filter it
1. Open **Explore**. Every open opportunity is here.
2. Type in **Search opportunities** to search titles, groups and topics.
3. Click a type (**Events**, **Petitions**, **Volunteering**, **Jobs**, **Other**) to narrow the list. Choose more than one, or **All** to clear them.
4. Click **Filters** to narrow by topic, when (**Next 7 days**, **Next 30 days**), place, **Free events only** and accessibility. The list updates as you choose, and the button at the bottom says how many results you'll see. Click it to close the panel.
5. Use **Sort by** to put the **Best match**, the **Soonest** or the **Newest** first.
6. Remove a single filter with the × on its tag, or click **Clear all**.

Notes:
- **Free events only** hides paid events. Volunteering, petitions and jobs are never hidden by it.
- Choosing an accessibility feature shows only events that offer it, because other types don't list accessibility.
- **Next 7 days** and **Next 30 days** only show opportunities with a date. Ongoing ones are left out.
- The address bar keeps your filters, so you can reload the page or send the link to someone.

## See the details first
Click a card's title (or anywhere on the card except the button) to open its details: when, where, cost, accessibility, skills, pay and so on, depending on the type. The button and **Share** are at the bottom. **Share** opens your device's share menu, or copies the link.

## Find what you opened
1. Open **Your opportunities**.
2. **Upcoming** lists what's still open, soonest first. Click the button to open the partner's page again.
3. **Past** lists what has ended, closed or is no longer available.
4. To take one off the list, click the × on its row. A message offers **Undo**.

**Your opportunities** shows what you opened, not whether you finished signing up. Signing up happens on the partner's own page, so we don't know the outcome.

## If something goes wrong
- **It says it couldn't add an opportunity.** The partner's page still opened. Click **Try again** in the message.
- **It says you've been signed out.** Click **Sign in** in the message.
- **You're offline.** You can still open the partner's page if it's already loaded. The opportunity is kept on this device only until you're back online.
- **The page didn't load.** Click **Try again**.
