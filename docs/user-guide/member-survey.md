# Membership survey (Ride for Refuge supporters)

You received an email from SDC offering a complimentary membership. These steps are for answering the survey it links to. It takes about two minutes, and you don't need to sign in.

## Share what you care about
1. Open the link in your invitation email.
2. Read the welcome, then choose **Get started**.
3. Check **Name** (optional) and **Email address** (required). If your email was filled in for you, change it if it isn't right. Choose **Next**.
4. Answer each of the five questions, or leave one blank. Choose **Next** after each one. The button says **Skip for now** until you pick something.
5. On the last question, write in the box if you like, then choose **Share my preferences**.
6. When you see "Thanks for sharing!", your answers are saved. You can close the page.

You can choose **Back** at any step to change an answer.

## Pick more than one answer
On "Which issues would you like to hear about or get involved in?" and "How would you like to be involved with SDC?", tick every card that fits. Choose **Other** to type your own answer. On the issues question, **I’m not sure yet** clears your other picks, and picking another card clears it.

## If your answers don't save
After **Share my preferences**, a red message says "We couldn’t save your answers." Your answers are still on the page. Check your connection and choose **Share my preferences** again.

## Unsubscribe
1. On the first screen, choose **Unsubscribe** at the bottom.
2. You'll see "You’re unsubscribed". SDC records that you chose this.

The invitation email also has an **Unsubscribe** button.
