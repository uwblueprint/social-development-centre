# Membership survey (Ride for Refuge supporters)

You received an email from SDC offering a complimentary membership. These steps are for answering
the survey. You answer it the first time you sign in, and it takes about two minutes.

## Sign in and answer the survey
1. Open the link in your invitation email and enter your email address to sign in.
2. SDC emails you a sign-in link. Open it. The survey opens on its own the first time.
3. You'll see an envelope addressed to you. Click it (or tap it on a phone) to open your letter.
4. Read the letter, then choose **Begin survey**.
5. On "Confirm your details", fill in **Name (optional)**. Your email is shown under **Signed in as**
   and can't be changed here — it's the address you signed in with. Choose **Next**.
6. Answer each of the five questions and choose **Next** after each one. Questions 1 to 4 need an
   answer: **Next** stays greyed out until you pick one.
7. On the last question, write in the box if you like (it's optional), then choose **Submit answers**.
8. When you see "Thanks for sharing!", your answers are saved. Choose **Go to SDC** to carry on.

You can choose **Back** at any step to change an answer.

## Pick more than one answer
On "Which issues would you like to hear about?" and "How would you like to be involved with SDC?",
tick every card that fits.

## Write your own answer
Every question with cards has an **Other** card at the bottom, below a line.
1. Choose **Other**. The card turns into a text box.
2. Type your answer. **Next** stays greyed out until the box has something in it.
3. To change your mind, untick **Other** (or, on a question where you pick one, choose another card).

## Unsubscribe
1. Choose **Unsubscribe** at the bottom of any survey screen.
2. On "Stop SDC invitations?", choose **Unsubscribe** to confirm, or **Keep me in** to go back to
   where you were.
3. You'll see "You’re unsubscribed". Choose **Sign out**.

## If your answers don't save
After **Submit answers**, a message says "We couldn’t save your answers." Your answers are still on
the page. Check your connection and choose **Submit answers** again.

If you close the page part-way through, nothing is saved, and the survey opens again the next time
you sign in.
