# Signing in as a paying member

For SDC paying members. Members sign in to see the members-only opportunities feed. There's no password.

## Sign in
1. Go to the **Sign in to SDC** page and enter your **Email address** (the one you use with SDC).
2. Click **Email me a sign-in link**.
3. Open the email ("Your SDC sign-in link") on the same device and browser, and click **Sign in to SDC**.

The link works once and expires in 15 minutes.

- **"Check your email":** the link is on its way. If it doesn't arrive, check your spam or promotions folder. **Resend link** works again after 60 seconds; until then it counts down. **Back to sign-in** lets you fix the address.
- **"This email isn't on our paying member list":** that address isn't a paying member. Click **Become a paying member** or **Join the newsletter**, or **Try a different email**. If you're already a member, contact SDC and they'll update your email.
- **"This link has expired":** click **Send a new link**, or **Use a different email**.

## Open an opportunity someone shared
Anyone can open a shared opportunity link without signing in.
1. Open the link. You see the opportunity and **Get opportunities like this by email**.
2. To get SDC's newsletter, enter your **Email address** and click **Subscribe**. You'll see **You're subscribed** (or **You're already subscribed** if you were on the list already).
3. Members: click **Member sign in** at the top. After you sign in from the email, you come back to the same opportunity.

If the opportunity has ended or was taken down, the page says **This opportunity is no longer available**; you can still subscribe.
