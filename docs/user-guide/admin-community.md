# Community

For SDC admins. Community holds everyone who gets SDC emails: **General members**, and **Paying members** who also get paid benefits. Each email address is one person.

## Find someone
1. Go to **Community** and choose **General members** or **Paying members**.
2. Type a name or email in the search box, then press **Enter** or click the search icon. Click the **×** to clear it.

People who unsubscribed appear at the end of **General members**, shown with a dimmed name and a small mail-off icon, and search always finds them.

## Add people or import a list
1. Open the tab for the kind of member you're adding (**General members** or **Paying members**).
2. Click **Add members**.
3. Paste one email or many, separated by commas or on separate lines. Names are optional; you can add them later.
4. Click **Continue** and review the summary: how many new members will be added and get a welcome email. If any addresses need attention, they show as their own line ("already members, skipped", "unsubscribed, not added", "invalid") — click a line to see which addresses.
5. Click **Add N members** to confirm, or **Back** to change the list.

New general members get the general welcome email. New paying members get the paying-member welcome; existing general members moved to paying get the upgrade email. Paying members are never moved down to general by an import, and unsubscribed people are never re-added.

## Edit someone's name or email
1. Click the person's row.
2. Open the **⋯** menu at the top of their panel and choose **Edit details**.
3. Change **Name** or **Email** and click **Save** (or **Cancel** to discard).

If the email already belongs to someone else, including someone who unsubscribed, you'll be told and nothing is saved.

## Copy someone's email
- In the table, hover their **Email** and click the copy icon that appears, or open their row and click the copy icon next to the email in the panel header. Either way, a **row's ⋯ menu → Copy email** does the same thing without opening the panel.

## Convert someone to a paying member
1. On **General members**, click the person.
2. Click **Convert to paying member** at the top of their panel (or choose it from the row's **⋯** menu without opening the panel).

They move to **Paying members** and get the upgrade email with their benefits and access link.

## Remove someone's paying access
1. On **Paying members**, click the person.
2. Click **Remove paying access** at the top of their panel (or from the row's **⋯** menu) and confirm.

Their paid benefits end right away and they get a notice. They stay a general member and keep getting general emails.

## Unsubscribe someone
1. Click the person, open the **⋯** menu, choose **Unsubscribe**, and confirm — or choose **Unsubscribe** from their row's **⋯** menu directly.

They stop getting all SDC emails. A paying member also loses paid access. Their record stays, shown with a dimmed name and a mail-off icon.

Bringing an unsubscribed person back isn't available yet; it's waiting on SDC's consent rules. Their panel's **⋯** menu shows a disabled **Restore email eligibility** with that reason.

## See someone's email history
1. Click the person to open their panel.
2. Click the **Emails** tab — it shows how many emails they've been sent.
3. Click an email to expand it and see the full message, or use **Expand all** / **Collapse all** above the list. A bounced email shows a **Bounced** badge.

## Export contacts
1. Click **Export**.
2. Choose who to include (the current tab by default, or **Everyone**) and whether to **Include unsubscribed members**.
3. Check the number of people shown, then click **Download CSV**.

The file has two columns: name and email.
