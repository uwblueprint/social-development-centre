## Design preferences
- Nothing moves that doesn't need to. Static labels and tags never animate in or out.
- No generic icon columns or placeholder UI. Use real-looking email, text-message and window UI.
- Line weights: 3px drawn strokes, 1.5px dividers and outlines, 8px skeleton bars, 4px hero lines. The constellation's connectors are the exception: 2px, solid white.
- Things that reveal in sequence use an even rhythm (people in the logo: one every 650ms), and overlap rather than wait.
- Quiet endings: the heart monitor goes grey, it doesn't alarm.
- Text messages look like text messages: tight padding, chat-bubble corners, blue for Suhanya's messages.
- Emails look like emails: no greeting, a sign-off, no subject line.
- Pace: slower and calmer than the first draft; avoid bursts next to slow motion.
